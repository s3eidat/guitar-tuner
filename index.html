Tuning

Tips
  • Click a string to hear its reference note and lock the tuner to it. Auto string picks the nearest one for you.
  • Pluck firmly and let the note ring; mute the other strings.
  • Tune up to the note: if you're sharp, go a little below and come back up.
  • Within ±5 cents is in tune; most ears can't hear less than that.

The microphone is only used while tuning is on, with echo cancellation and noise suppression switched off so the real pitch comes through.
